Design assumptions:

- Design for Hong Kong Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013
- Do NOT support RC design of Cantilever beam
- The beam is under sagging stress, and without pattern loads

- Max. 4 rows of mainbar
- No more than one type of mainbar per beam, eg. T25
- No more than one type of shearbar per beam, eg. T10
- All mainbar rows contain same nos. of rebar 

- Do NOT consider torsional design
- Do NOT consider side bar
- Do NOT consider crack width
- Do NOT consider curtailment
- Do NOT consider lapping of mainbar
